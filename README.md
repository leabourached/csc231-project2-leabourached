# csc231-project2-leabourached



**game aesthetic**



* game title: Fern \& the fading flame
* main original character (player): Fern, a red fox.
* environment: forest, night setting
* enemies: wandering spirits
* storyline/backstory:

Fern is a young red fox from a long line of forest guardians. His ancestors built shrines throughout the forest, each lit by a pyrka, a tiny fire spirit that keeps the evil spirits away. But something scared the pyrkas off into the night, and the shrines went dark. Now Fern must travel through the forest, gather the lost pyrkas, and return them to their shrines before dawn.





signature move: double jump

attack: lantern swing, illuminating lantern attack

collectibles: Pyrkas (fire spirits, Greek "pyr" for fire)

checkpoint: 4 shrines (fern brings back 3 pyrkas to each shrine), relighting all the shrine ends the level.

hazards: thorns \& the shadow monsters (evil spirits)

(double jump to get away from the thorns, jump on platforms and reach pyrkas + the attacks can be done in the air)



Controls



\- A / D: Move left and right.

\- Space: Jump and double jump.

\- Left mouse button: Lantern staff swing (against flying shadow monsters).

\- Right mouse button: Illuminating lantern attack (against ground shadow monsters).

\- Escape: Pause the game.



\## Game Objective



Collect all 12 Pyrkas and restore the four shrines. Each shrine requires three Pyrkas to be activated and serves as a checkpoint. Restore all four shrines and reach the end of the level to win.



Features



\- Original character and enemy designs.

\- Character and enemy animations.

\- Parallax forest background.

\- Dynamic lantern lighting and illumination effects.

\- Health system and checkpoint revival.

\- Two different enemy types with unique attack mechanics.

\- Title screen, game information, pause menu, death screen and victory screen.

\- Background ambience and sound effects.

\- Separate mute options for ambience and sound effects.
